Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data the Intrigue app ("Intrigue", "the app") handles, where that data is kept, who can see it, and the rights you have over it. It applies to the Intrigue app for iPhone. Please read it together with our Terms of Use.

2. The short version

  • We, the developer, do not run any server for Intrigue and we never receive what you write in the app. We have no access to your missions, hunches, guesses, names or scores.
  • Your missions, hunches and guesses are stored only on your iPhone until you and your partner hold the Debrief.
  • The information the game must share with your partner travels through Apple's iCloud, in a private space that only you and your partner can open.
  • Intrigue contains no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party software development kits. We do not sell or rent personal data, and we do not use it for profiling.

3. Data stored only on your iPhone

The following data is created and kept on your device, in the app's own storage. It does not leave your device except as described in section 4.

  • Your game: the missions dealt to you, whether you completed or vetoed them, your hunches, the hunches you chose as guesses, the ratings you gave, and the results of past rounds.
  • Your settings: the name you chose for yourself, your partner's name as they shared it, your notification choices, whether the app lock is switched on, and which screens you have already seen.
  • Notification timing: the times you opened the app in the last fourteen app openings, used only to choose when to show the partner activity notification. These times never leave your device.

Deleting the app from your iPhone deletes this data. It cannot be recovered afterwards, because no copy exists anywhere else.

4. Data shared with your partner through iCloud

To let two phones play one game, Intrigue uses Apple's CloudKit service. The partner who starts the game creates a private storage area in their own iCloud account and shares it, by name, with exactly one other person: the partner they invite. Only those two Apple accounts can read or write it. We, the developer, cannot read it.

The following data is placed in that shared area:

  • Pairing: a random identifier for your couple, and a pseudonymous identifier that Apple assigns to each iCloud account. This identifier does not contain your name, email address or phone number.
  • Names: the display name each of you chose for yourself.
  • Round status: the round number and its codename, when the round started, when the Debrief is scheduled, whether the round is paused and by whom, the relationship mode you selected ("living together" or "long distance"), and whether each of you has said you are ready for the Debrief.
  • Progress counts: how many hunches you have logged and how many of your missions you have completed. Only the numbers are shared, never the content.
  • Debrief presence: a timestamp showing that you have the Debrief screen open, so the two phones can begin together.
  • The Debrief: when you both join the Debrief, your missions (their titles, descriptions, point values and whether you completed them) and the hunches you chose as guesses are shared with your partner, together with the ratings each of you gives and the final scores of the round.

Data in the shared area remains in the iCloud account of the partner who started the game until it is deleted as described in section 8.

5. Inviting your partner

To invite your partner, you enter the email address or phone number of their iCloud account. The app passes it to Apple only so that Apple can find that account and limit the invitation to it. The app does not store the email address or phone number, and we never receive it. You then send the invitation link yourself, through any app you choose.

Apple may show the participants of a shared iCloud area their iCloud names. This is a function of iCloud and is governed by Apple's terms.

6. Data we do not collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or advertising identifiers.
  • Face ID, Touch ID and your device passcode are checked by iOS. The app only learns whether the check succeeded. It never receives biometric data.
  • We do not use cookies, web trackers, advertising networks or analytics services.

If you have agreed in your iPhone's settings to share analytics or crash reports with app developers, Apple may provide us with aggregated, anonymised usage statistics and crash reports about the app. Apple controls that sharing, and you can switch it off at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

7. Purposes and legal basis

We process the data described above only to provide the game you asked to play: to deal and keep your missions, record your hunches, keep the two phones in step, hold the Debrief and keep your history. The legal basis is the performance of our agreement with you (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Notifications are sent only if you allow them in iOS and switch them on in the app. You can withdraw that choice at any time in the app's Settings or in iOS Settings.

8. How long data is kept, and deleting it

  • On your iPhone: until you delete your account in the app, or delete the app.
  • In the shared iCloud area: until the partner who started the game deletes their account in the app, which deletes the whole shared area for both of you. When the partner who joined deletes their account, the app removes their name from the shared area and ends their access to it. Records of rounds already played remain in the other partner's iCloud account and on their phone, because they also belong to that partner's game history.
  • Your partner's phone: what your partner has already seen (for example, missions revealed at a Debrief) remains on their phone. We cannot delete data from another person's device.

To delete your account, open the You tab › Settings › Delete account. If iCloud cannot be reached, nothing is deleted and the app tells you, so that you are never led to believe your data is gone when it is not.

9. Recipients and transfers

Apple Inc. and its affiliates provide iCloud, CloudKit and push notification delivery. Apple processes the data in section 4 under your own agreement with Apple and Apple's Privacy Policy (apple.com/legal/privacy). Apple may store and process data in countries other than your own, including the United States. We do not transfer personal data to anyone else.

10. Security

The shared area can be opened only by the two invited Apple accounts, and data travels encrypted between your phone and iCloud. You can lock Intrigue with Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode, and the app hides its content in the app switcher. Data exports are created with the strongest file protection iOS offers and are deleted from the app's temporary storage when you close the export screen. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and you are responsible for keeping your device, passcode and Apple account secure.

11. Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws you have the right to access your data, to have it corrected, to have it erased, to restrict its processing, to object to its processing, and to receive it in a portable format. Because your data never reaches us, you exercise these rights directly in the app:

  • Access and portability: You tab › Settings › Export my data creates a file containing all of your own game data held on your iPhone.
  • Correction: You tab › Profile & Pairing lets you change your name.
  • Erasure: You tab › Settings › Delete account.

You can also contact us at the email address in section 1. You have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the country where you live or work.

12. Age

Intrigue is intended for adults. It is not directed at anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly process the data of anyone under 18.
